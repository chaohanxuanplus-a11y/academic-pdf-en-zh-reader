# Translation and contextual explanation

Translate every required logical unit completely and in order, including title, abstract, keywords, body, captions and footnotes. Preserve numbers, units, inequalities, citations, uncertainty, negation and conclusion strength. The program excludes reference entries from required units. Keep a small paper-wide terminology list to maintain consistency across paragraph batches. Do not retranslate a successful batch for a layout failure.

Add meaningful vocabulary at its first useful occurrence: English expression, contextual Chinese meaning and a concise explanation when needed. Repeated terms do not require repeated notes. Mark indispensable terms essential and discretionary language teaching optional; avoid a quota of filler notes per paragraph.

For each figure/table, inspect the object and caption alongside the paragraphs citing it. Explain the question being tested, axes/units or table columns, groups/control, main comparison or trend, and what it contributes to the surrounding argument. For multi-panel figures, identify panels and their relation; for tables, identify major contrasts and footnotes/statistics that affect interpretation. Preserve representative values and experimental conditions when visible. Distinguish author interpretation from a cautious inference; do not infer causation or significance from appearance alone.

Supply a complete explanation plus a shorter core version containing the principal finding, key comparison and limitations. Bind statements to actual source excerpts. When an object detail is unreadable, state the exact missing detail and explain only what the caption/text supports; do not invent axis values or silently omit the object. No routine web lookup or external service is needed.

Review ambiguous terminology, complex sentences, quantitative statements and flagged units. Record the actual checked IDs. Correct definite errors locally. A heuristic language flag may be resolved with a text-bound reason; exact numeric/citation errors require correction. Retain justified unresolved source ambiguity with the existing visible marker.
